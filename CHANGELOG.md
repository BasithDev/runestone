# Changelog

Each `## vX.Y.Z` section becomes the GitHub release notes and is shown in the
app's "Update available" pop-up.

## v1.0.0

First public release.

### Tracking
- Track movies, series and anime: Watching, Caught Up, On Hold, Dropped, Rewatching, Completed
- Episode progress with per-season marking and quick +1 from Home
- One-tap tracking from search results: watched, caught up, or "watching from" any episode

### Discover
- Search with history, genre, language, rating, year and sort filters
- Browse TMDb by filters (e.g. Malayalam thrillers rated 7+)
- Upcoming movies in India with countdowns

### Alerts (India)
- Release reminders 7 days before and on release day
- OTT release dates and "now streaming" alerts
- New season announcements and premiere dates

### Stats & lists
- Stats with activity heatmap, year in review, top genres and directors, and your full watched library
- Watchlist with priorities, notes, grid/list views and an "Up Next" row

### Data
- Backup & restore to a file (Drive, Files, WhatsApp)
- In-app update check with changelog
